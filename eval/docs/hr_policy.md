# HR Policy

## Annual Leave

Employees get annual leave of 25 days per calendar year. Unused days may be carried over until 31 March of the following year. Leave requests must be approved by the line manager at least 5 working days in advance.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days of absence. Employees must notify their manager before 9:00 on the first day of absence.

## Remote Work

Remote work is allowed two days per week. Employees cannot work remotely from another country without written approval from HR. The company provides a laptop and a monthly internet allowance of 30 EUR.

## Expenses

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Hotel | 120 EUR per night |
| Meals | 40 EUR per day |
| Taxi | Only with manager approval |

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip.
